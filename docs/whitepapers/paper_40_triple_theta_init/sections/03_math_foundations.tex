\section{Mathematical Foundations}
Let $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{out}} \times d_{\text{in}}}$ be a weight tensor with $N = d_{\text{out}} \times d_{\text{in}}$ total elements. Let $i \in \{0, 1, \dots, N-1\}$ denote the flattened element index.

\subsection{Triple-Theta Fractal Equation}

\begin{equation}
    p_i = \lfloor i \cdot \phi \rceil \bmod 100
\end{equation}
where $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.61803398875$.

The golden exponent mapping produces the continuous scale sequence:
\begin{equation}
    m_i = (\phi^{p_i}) \bmod 9.0
\end{equation}

\subsection{Biological Exclusion Gate Filtering}
The scalar scale $m_i$ is passed through the biological exclusion operator $\mathcal{G}_{\text{excl}}$:
\begin{equation}
    g_i = \mathcal{G}_{\text{excl}}(m_i)
\end{equation}
The exclusion gate returns zero whenever $m_i$ falls into chaotic modular basins, retaining active multipliers only for stable residue classes $\{1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8\}$.

The final initialized weight value $w_i$ is given by:
\begin{equation}
    w_i = w_{\text{raw}, i} \cdot \frac{g_i}{1000.0}
\end{equation}
The division by $1000.0$ normalizes variance bounds, preserving stability across deep layer stacks.
