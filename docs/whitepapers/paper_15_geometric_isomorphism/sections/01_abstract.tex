By precomputing a static block-diagonal 2D rotational-scale transformation matrix $M_{\text{iso}} \in \mathbb{R}^{D \times D}$ with determinant $\det(M_{\text{iso}}) = 1.0$ and condition number $\kappa(M_{\text{iso}}) = \phi^2 \approx 2.61803398875$, our protocol guarantees strict measure preservation, maximum signal-to-noise ratio, and complete TTT-7 stability across deep network layers.
