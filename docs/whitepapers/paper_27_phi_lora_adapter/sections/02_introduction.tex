\section{Introduction}
Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) has become the dominant paradigm for adapting large-scale foundation models to specialized tasks. Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) decomposes weight update matrices into low-rank factorization factors $\Delta W = B A$, where $A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times d_{in}}$ and $B \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{out} \times r}$ with rank $r \ll \min(d_{in}, d_{out})$.

Despite its efficiency, standard LoRA suffers from rank truncation artifacts and subspace mismatch when applied to non-Euclidean representation spaces. The \textbf{Nexus Resonance Codex (NRC)} solves these bottlenecks through golden ratio scaling ($\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.618034$) and shard folding.

In this work, we present \textbf{PhiInfinityLosslessLoRA}, which integrates golden scaling and double \texttt{PhiInfinityShardFolding} operators to achieve lossless low-rank adaptation.

\subsection{Key Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Golden Ratio Modulation}: Replacement of standard $\alpha/r$ scaling with $\phi$-bounded factor $\gamma = \frac{\alpha}{r} \cdot \phi$.
    \item \textbf{Double Shard Compression}: Dual application of \texttt{PhiInfinityShardFolding} on rank-$A$ and rank-$B$ intermediate features.
    \item \textbf{Subspace Alignment Proof}: Formal proof demonstrating singular value stabilization and elimination of low-rank interference.
\end{enumerate}
