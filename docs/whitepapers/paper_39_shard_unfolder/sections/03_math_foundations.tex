\section{Mathematical Foundations}
Let $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times L \times D}$ denote an active key-value tensor. When folded at depth $N \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$, the compressed state is $X_{\text{folded}} = X \cdot \phi^{-N}$.

\subsection{Algebraic Unfolding Equation}
The restoration operator $\mathcal{U}_N: \mathbb{R}^{B \times L \times D} \to \mathbb{R}^{B \times L \times D}$ applies the inverse golden expansion factor $\alpha_N = \phi^N$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{U}_N(X_{\text{folded}}) = X_{\text{folded}} \cdot \phi^N
\end{equation}
where $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.61803398875$.

\subsection{Composition Identity & Perfect Recovery}
Composing the folding operator $\mathcal{F}_N(X) = X \cdot \phi^{-N}$ with the unfolding operator $\mathcal{U}_N$ yields the exact identity map:
\begin{equation}
    (\mathcal{U}_N \circ \mathcal{F}_N)(X) = \mathcal{U}_N(X \cdot \phi^{-N}) = (X \cdot \phi^{-N}) \cdot \phi^N = X \cdot (\phi^{-N} \cdot \phi^N) = X \cdot 1 = X
\end{equation}
Since matrix multiplication by a scalar is distributive and associative, perfect reconstruction holds identically across all tensor dimensions $B, L, D$.
