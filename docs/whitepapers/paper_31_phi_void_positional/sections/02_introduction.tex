The ability to effectively embed sequences in high-dimensional spaces is a crucial component of many neural network architectures. Traditional positional encoding schemes, such as sinusoidal positional encoding, utilize arbitrary frequencies to create a fixed sequence of embeddings. However, these approaches often fail to capture the underlying structure of the data, leading to suboptimal performance. In this paper, we propose a novel approach to positional encoding, Phi-Void Positional Encoding, which leverages the mathematical properties of the Golden Ratio (phi) to create a more efficient and effective way of embedding sequences.
