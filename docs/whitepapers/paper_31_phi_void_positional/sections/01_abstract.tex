\begin{abstractbox}
We introduce a novel positional encoding scheme, Phi-Void Positional Encoding, which leverages the mathematical properties of the Golden Ratio (phi) to create a more efficient and effective way of embedding sequences in high-dimensional spaces. By utilizing the phi^6 void resonance, our approach replaces arbitrary sinusoidal frequencies with native Golden Ratio wave propagation limits, allowing sequences to align topologically to perfect physical spaces. Our method is demonstrated to be particularly useful for high-dimensional manifold projections, such as 2048D and 8192D spaces, and is shown to be compliant with TTT-7 stability digital root audits. We provide a comprehensive mathematical foundation for our approach, including digital root definitions and a formal proof of the TTT-7 theorem.
\end{abstractbox}
