Traditional methods for protein structure prediction rely on empirical models and energy functions, which often struggle to accurately capture the complex interactions governing protein folding. The Nexus Resonance Codex (NRC) Protein Folding Engine addresses these limitations by introducing a novel mathematical framework that leverages high-dimensional manifold projections and digital root audits to enhance protein conformation prediction accuracy.
