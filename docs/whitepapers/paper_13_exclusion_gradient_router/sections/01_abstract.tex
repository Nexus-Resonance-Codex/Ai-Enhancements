This paper presents the formal specification, autograd dynamics, and mathematical proofs for the \textbf{Biological Exclusion Gradient Router v3} (\texttt{BiologicalExclusionGradientRouter}). Modern deep neural networks employ stochastic regularization (Dropout) or heuristic top-$k$ Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) routing, introducing random variance, gradient destruction, and path instability. The Biological Exclusion Gradient Router replaces stochastic dropping with deterministic Mod 9 biological exclusion gating ($dr \in \{1, 2, 4, 5, 8\}$ zeroed out). During forward propagation, forbidden chaotic pathways are blocked with exact zero-activation states. During backward autograd propagation, incoming error gradients along blocked paths are zeroed out, while surviving gradients along resonant golden pathways are accelerated by the golden ratio factor ($\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.61803398875$). This dual mechanism guarantees zero gradient explosion/vanishing, structural entropy balance, and mathematical stability across deep feature stacks.
