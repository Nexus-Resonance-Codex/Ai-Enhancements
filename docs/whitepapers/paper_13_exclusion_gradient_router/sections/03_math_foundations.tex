\section{Mathematical Foundations}

Let $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ be an input tensor entering the router layer.

\subsection{Forward Exclusion Operator}
The Mod 9 exclusion gate $\mathcal{G}_{\text{excl}}$ maps $\mathbf{x} \to \mathbf{y}$ as:
\begin{equation}
y_i = \mathcal{G}_{\text{excl}}(x_i) = \begin{cases}
0.0, & \text{if } dr(\lfloor |x_i| \cdot 10^7 \rfloor) \in \{1, 2, 4, 5, 8\} \\
x_i, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
The pass-through binary mask $M \in \{0, 1\}^D$ is extracted directly:
\begin{equation}
M_i = \mathbb{I}(y_i \neq 0.0)
\end{equation}
$M$ is saved in the Autograd context tensor buffer.

\subsection{Backward Resonant Autograd Mapping}
Let $\mathbf{g}_{\text{out}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{y}}$ be the upstream loss gradient. The backward transformation computes the downstream gradient $\mathbf{g}_{\text{in}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{x}}$ via:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{g}_{\text{routed}} = \mathbf{g}_{\text{out}} \odot M
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{g}_{\text{in}} = \mathbf{g}_{\text{routed}} \cdot \phi
\end{equation}
where $\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.618033988749895$.

\subsection{Energy Preservation Factor}
The average pass-through ratio under Mod 9 exclusion is $\alpha = \frac{4}{9} \approx 0.4444$. The expected gradient energy ratio is:
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{E}[\|\mathbf{g}_{\text{in}}\|_2^2] = \phi^2 \cdot \mathbb{E}[\|\mathbf{g}_{\text{out}} \odot M\|_2^2] \approx 2.61803 \cdot \frac{4}{9} \cdot \|\mathbf{g}_{\text{out}}\|_2^2 \approx 1.16357 \cdot \|\mathbf{g}_{\text{out}}\|_2^2
\end{equation}
This mild $\sim 1.16\times$ energy boost ensures robust error signal flow along surviving golden pathways.
