\begin{abstractbox}
This whitepaper delivers the theoretical derivation, PyTorch implementation, and mathematical proofs for the \textbf{Floor-Sinh Activation Regularizer} (\texttt{FloorSinhActivation}). Traditional activation functions—including ReLU \cite{nair2010rectified}, GELU \cite{hendrycks2016gaussian}, and Swish \cite{ramachandran2017searching}—suffer from severe geometric limitations: ReLU completely clamps negative activation manifolds to zero (causing neuron death), while GELU and Swish introduce unbounded positive growth or shallow negative dying regimes. The Floor-Sinh module resolves these pathologies by combining hyperbolic sine ($\sinh(x) = \frac{e^x - e^{-x}}{2}$) non-linearities with a rigid physical floor constraint $c_{\text{floor}} = -1.0$ via $\text{clamp}(\sinh(x), \text{min}=c_{\text{floor}})$. This hybrid activation maintains continuous hyperbolic curvature in positive and moderate negative domains, while establishing an absolute physical lower bound that prevents dimensional collapse under extreme negative error correlations.
\end{abstractbox}
