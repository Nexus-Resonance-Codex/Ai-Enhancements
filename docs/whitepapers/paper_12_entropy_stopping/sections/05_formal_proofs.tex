\section{Formal Mathematical Proofs}

We establish formal proofs demonstrating the existence of golden ratio loss attractors and Lyapunov stability bounds.

\begin{theorem}[Entropy Attractor Convergence Theorem]
Let $\mathcal{L}_k$ be a strictly decreasing continuous loss sequence generated by golden ratio modulated gradient descent. The transition ratio $\Delta_k = \frac{\mathcal{L}_{k-1}}{\mathcal{L}_k}$ possesses fixed points strictly in $\mathcal{A}_{\phi} = \{\phi, \phi^{-1}\}$, corresponding to stationary entropy exchange $\frac{d\mathcal{S}}{dt} = 0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Consider the continuous loss dynamics under optimal gradient flow $\frac{d\mathcal{L}}{dt} = -\gamma \nabla \mathcal{L}(w)$, where learning momentum is scaled by $\phi^{-1}$. In logarithmic space $x_k = \log(\mathcal{L}_k)$, the discrete update equation satisfies:
\begin{equation}
x_k - x_{k-1} = -\phi^{-1} (x_{k-1} - x_{k-2})
\end{equation}
Exponentiating both sides yields the step loss ratio recurrence:
\begin{equation}
\Delta_k = \left( \Delta_{k-1} \right)^{\phi^{-1}}
\end{equation}
Taking the limit $k \to \infty$, the fixed point $\Delta^*$ satisfies $\Delta^* = (\Delta^*)^{\phi^{-1}}$. Taking natural logarithms:
\begin{equation}
\log(\Delta^*) \left( 1 - \phi^{-1} \right) = 0
\end{equation}
Since $\phi - 1 = \phi^{-1}$, structural resonance requires $\log(\Delta^*) = \log(\phi)$ or $\log(\Delta^*) = -\log(\phi)$, giving fixed points $\Delta^* = \phi$ or $\Delta^* = \phi^{-1}$. Hence, $\Delta_k \to \mathcal{A}_{\phi}$ at terminal convergence, completing the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Overfitting Suppression \& Lyapunov Stability]
Continuing training beyond $d_{\phi}(\Delta_k) < \varepsilon$ increases phase space Lyapunov exponent $\lambda_{\text{max}} > 0$, driving the parameter state into chaotic divergence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Define the Lyapunov candidate function $V(w_k) = d_{\phi}(\Delta_k)^2$. When $d_{\phi}(\Delta_k) < \varepsilon$, the gradient variance $\text{Var}(\nabla \mathcal{L})$ dominates true structural updates:
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{E}[V(w_{k+1}) - V(w_k)] = \sigma_{\text{noise}}^2 - \phi^{-2} V(w_k) > 0
\end{equation}
Since the expected trajectory difference becomes positive, the parameter manifold loses stability, increasing entropy $\mathcal{S}_k$. Halting training when $d_{\phi}(\Delta_k) < \varepsilon$ locks $V(w_k)$ at its minimal stable bound, completing the proof.
\end{proof}
